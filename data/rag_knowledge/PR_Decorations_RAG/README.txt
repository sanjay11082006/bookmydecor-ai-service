PR DECORATIONS — RAG KNOWLEDGE BASE

This folder is the pre-RAG knowledge base for the PR Decorations chatbot.

IMPORTANT:
- Use confirmed business information only.
- Do not invent missing information.
- Prices are starting prices unless the source explicitly gives a range/flat rate.
- Final pricing varies according to customer requirements and selected add-ons.
- Live booking availability should come from the booking database, not static RAG documents.
- Real photos should be added after collection from the business.

FOLDERS:
01_business      Business identity and profile
02_services      One consistent file per service
03_pricing       Add-on and pricing rules
04_booking       Booking policies and availability rules
05_faq           Confirmed chatbot Q&A
06_gallery       Real business images and future metadata
07_website       Verified website usage instructions

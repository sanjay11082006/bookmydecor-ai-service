PR DECORATIONS — BOOKING RULES AND POLICIES


ADVANCE BOOKING:

Customers should book at least 1 month in advance,
especially during busy or peak seasons.


CALENDAR AVAILABILITY:

The booking calendar uses the following indication:

- White = Available
- Red = Already Booked

The chatbot should use the live booking system/database
to determine whether a specific date is currently available.

The static RAG knowledge should not be used to answer
real-time availability questions.


ADVANCE PAYMENT:

A 25% advance payment is required to confirm any booking.


CANCELLATION POLICY:

If a booking is cancelled, the advance payment already paid
is NOT refunded.


ADD-ON FLEXIBILITY:

Add-ons can be combined with services based on the
customer's requirements.

Examples include:
- Cold Fires
- Smoke Effect
- Paper Shots
- Car Entry
- Royal Entry
- Catering
- Flowers
- Traveling / Travel Charges


PHOTOGRAPHY:

PR Decorations does not provide photography directly.

A known/trusted external photographer can be referred
to the customer on request.


PRICING RULE:

All service prices listed by PR Decorations are starting
prices unless a specific price or range is stated.

Final pricing varies according to:
- Customized themes
- Customer requirements
- Decoration preferences
- Guest count where applicable
- Selected add-ons
- Travel distance where applicable


UNCONFIRMED INFORMATION:

The following information has not yet been confirmed
by the business:

- Accepted payment methods
- Discount policy
- Rescheduling policy
- Exact service/travel area


CHATBOT RULE:

If a customer asks about information that has not been
confirmed, the chatbot should not guess.

The chatbot should respond that the information is not
currently available and ask the customer to contact
PR Decorations directly.


SOURCE:

Information collected directly from the PR Decorations owner.


STATUS:

Confirmed business rules, except the items explicitly
marked as unconfirmed.

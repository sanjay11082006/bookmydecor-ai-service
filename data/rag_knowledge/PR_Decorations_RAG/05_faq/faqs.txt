PR DECORATIONS — CHATBOT FAQ KNOWLEDGE

Q: What is the starting price for a marriage decoration package?
A: Starting from ₹80,000, up to ₹3,00,000 depending on requirements. The basic plan includes Haldi, Sangeet, and Bride-to-be setup.

Q: What is included in the basic birthday package?
A: For ₹15,000 starting price, the package includes balloon theme decoration, name decoration, and entries. Add-ons may cost extra.

Q: How much advance payment do I need to pay?
A: 25% of the total package price is required as advance to confirm the booking.

Q: Is the advance payment refundable if I cancel?
A: No. The advance payment is non-refundable if the booking is cancelled.

Q: How far in advance should I book?
A: At least 1 month in advance is recommended, especially during busy/peak season.

Q: How do I know if my preferred date is available?
A: Check the booking calendar. Available dates are shown in white and already-booked dates are shown in red.

Q: Do you provide photography services?
A: Not directly. PR Decorations can refer a known, trusted photographer on request.

Q: Can I customize a package or add extra items?
A: Yes. Add-ons can be added to services based on customer requirements.

Q: Do prices change based on location or travel distance?
A: Yes. Traveling charges range from ₹1,500 to ₹50,000 depending on distance to the venue.

Q: What are your operating hours?
A: Open every day from 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM.

Q: Do you use real or artificial flowers?
A: Both are available. Original flowers are ₹20,000 per session, duplicate/artificial flowers are ₹5,000, and mixed flowers are ₹15,000.

Q: Do you provide catering services?
A: Yes. Catering is available from ₹10,000 to ₹10,00,000 depending on guest count and menu.

Q: What payment methods do you accept?
A: Both offline and online payments are accepted. For online payments, any UPI or digital payment method commonly used in India is accepted (e.g., PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm, or any other UPI app). Offline cash payments are also accepted.

Q: Do you offer discounts for early booking or referrals?
A: I don't know. Please contact PR Decorations directly. This information has not yet been confirmed by the business.
